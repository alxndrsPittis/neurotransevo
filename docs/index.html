1 · Cell-type-averaged expression

For each atlas, UMI counts are summed per annotated cell type and divided by the number of cells of that type. The same six matrices feed every expression figure and the coregulon analysis. R/matrices.R

2 · Amine-metabolism coregulons Fig. 8, S11–S14

Seeds are the members of eight families involved in amine biosynthesis, vesicular transport and reuptake (Acetyltransf, Biopterin_H, Pyridoxal-AADC, DOMON, P450-CYP2D6, SLC18, SLC5, SNF). For each seed, Pearson r and a two-tailed p-value are computed across cell types against all expressed genes; p-values are Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted per seed within the candidate pool (GO:0009308 “amine metabolic process” genes plus seeds).

A gene joins the coregulon when r ≥ 0.5 and adjusted p ≤ 0.05 for at least one seed that has a non-self partner; the coregulon is the union of GO:0009308 partners and these active seeds.

3 · Gene-family expression heatmaps Fig. 4D–E, 5C–D, 7, S4–S10

Rows are the curated members of each family (table order; hand-curated phylogeny order where published), z-scored across cell types. Columns are ordered by complete-linkage clustering of 1 − Pearson r on the whole transcriptome, with neuronal cell types in a right-hand block, so every panel of a species shares the same column order. Fig. 5C–D add the Pearson correlation (p ≤ 0.05) of each nAChR subunit with Rapsyn-related genes.

5 · Gene-family phylogenies Fig. 2–5, S1–S2

  1. Pfam HMM search (hmmsearch --cut_ga) against 68 eukaryotic proteomes and UniProt prokaryotic reference proteomes; above 300 prokaryotic hits, at most 100 per phylum.
  2. MFS and GPCR-A superfamilies: all-vs-all BLASTP (E ≤ 1e-5) clustered with MCL (inflation 1.4 and 6.0).
  3. MAFFT E-INS-i / L-INS-i, columns with >90% gaps removed, IQ-TREE (LG models, ultrafast bootstrap); for >1,000 sequences Clustal Omega, >99% gap filter and FastTree, followed by re-analysis of the clade of interest.
  4. Trees annotated and drawn with ETE (python/phylogenies/plot_gene_tree.py).
